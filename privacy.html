Website privacy

Clear, useful, and respectful.

This policy explains what happens when you visit kestoralabs.com. It covers this studio website only; each Kestora app has its own privacy policy linked from its App Store listing.

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Who is responsible

Kestora Labs is operated by Kenneth M. S. De Geest in Spain. For privacy questions, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Essential website data

The site is hosted with GitHub Pages. Like most hosting services, GitHub may process technical request data needed to deliver and secure the website, such as an IP address, browser information, requested pages, and timestamps. Kestora Labs does not use this hosting data to build personal profiles.

Optional analytics

If you choose “Accept analytics,” the site loads Google Analytics 4. It helps us understand overall use of the site, including page views, approximate location, device and browser type, scrolling, and clicks to App Store pages.

  • Analytics is off by default and does not load before you consent.
  • Advertising storage, advertising personalization, and Google Signals are disabled.
  • We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, or other directly identifying information to Google Analytics.
  • Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can remember it.

Google Ireland Limited acts as our analytics service provider. Google may process data on servers outside the European Economic Area using its applicable international transfer safeguards. Learn more in Google’s privacy policy.

Legal basis and retention

Optional analytics is processed only with your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR. Aggregated Analytics event data is configured to be retained for up to 14 months. You can withdraw consent at any time through Cookie settings; this stops future analytics collection on this browser.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your local supervisory authority. Contact us at the email above to make a request.

Change your choice

Reopen the privacy controls to accept analytics or switch back to essential-only use.
