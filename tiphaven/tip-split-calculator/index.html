How this tip sharing calculator works

Start with the amount available for distribution. Assign a percentage to each role, and make sure the percentages total 100%. The calculator divides each role’s allocation equally among the number of people in that role.

each person = distributable tips × role percentage ÷ people in role

Tip split calculator vs tip pool calculator

Role splitBest for percentages

Use this page when roles receive fixed shares of the total.

Hours poolBest for time worked

Use the tip pool calculator when each worked hour carries the same value.

A calculator does not set the policy

The distribution method should be communicated clearly and follow applicable rules. Under U.S. federal law, the type of employees who may participate can depend on whether the employer takes a tip credit. Managers and supervisors generally may not receive employees’ pooled tips.

Frequently asked questions

What if the percentages do not total 100%?+

The calculator flags the difference so the whole distributable amount is not accidentally over- or under-allocated.

Can roles have different numbers of people?+

Yes. Each role allocation is divided by the number of people entered for that role.

Is this payroll software?+

No. It is an arithmetic tool and does not determine eligibility, withholding or legal compliance.
