Average bartender salary with tips: use the median carefully

National wage data are a useful reference, but the shift, venue and tip-sharing setup determine what your own time pays.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of TipHaven · Updated November 11, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick answerWhat is the average bartender wage with tips?

The BLS reports a median bartender wage of $16.51 per hour in May 2025, including tips. The listed annual equivalent is $34,340, but part-time work and variable schedules are common.

A median is the midpoint: half of workers earned more and half earned less. It is not a forecast for a particular venue or schedule.

The national range is wide

Bartender earnings vary across locations, industries, venue prices, scheduling, customer volume and tip-sharing rules. National figures combine many very different jobs.

What changes bartender earnings?

Shift

Weekend nights and slow weekday lunches can behave differently.

Venue

Menu prices, service model and customer volume affect tips.

Tip-out

Support-staff distributions reduce gross tips to net tips.

Hours

Opening, closing and side-work time change hourly earnings.

Wage

Direct wages and applicable tip-credit rules vary.

Measure your own true hourly earnings

For each shift, add direct wages to tips kept after tip-out, then divide by all hours worked. Compare several weeks rather than judging the job from one unusually strong or weak shift.

StepFormula
Net tipsGross tips − tip-out
Total shift earningsNet tips + direct wages
True hourly earningsTotal shift earnings ÷ hours worked
