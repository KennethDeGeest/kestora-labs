Average bartender salary with tips: use the median carefully.

National wage data are a useful reference, but the shift, venue and tip-sharing setup determine what your own time pays.

What the latest national data say

The BLS reports a median bartender wage of $16.51 per hour in May 2025.

The wage data include tips. The annual equivalent listed is $34,340, but part-time work and variable schedules are common.

The median is the midpoint: half of bartenders earned more and half earned less. It is not the same as an average tip amount and it cannot predict an individual bar, city or schedule.

The national range is wide

BLS reports that the lowest 10% earned under $9.67 per hour while the highest 10% earned above $35.47. That spread reflects major differences in venues, location, schedules, experience and tip volume.

What changes bartender earnings?

  • VenueHotels, full-service restaurants, drinking places and event venues have different traffic and price levels.
  • ShiftLate nights, weekends, events and seasonality can change both sales and hours.
  • Tip sharingServer tip-out, bar-back shares and pooled tips affect the amount retained.

Measure your own true hourly earnings

(direct wages + tips received − tip-out) ÷ total hours

Include setup, closing and side-work time. Compare several weeks instead of judging the job from one exceptional Saturday or one quiet Tuesday.
