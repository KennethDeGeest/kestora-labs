Six things a server tip tracker should do well

1. Log a shift quickly
Cash, card, hours, wages and tip-out should not require a spreadsheet.Essential
2. Show the real result
Net tips and total earnings matter more than gross tips alone.Essential
3. Compare time fairly
Hourly earnings help compare shifts of different lengths.High value
4. Respect sensitive data
Understand where records live and whether an account is required.Essential
5. Let you leave
Useful exports and backups reduce lock-in.Important
6. Use clear pricing
Know whether the app is free, subscription-based or a one-time purchase.Important

A practical pre-download checklist

  • Can it record cash tips, card tips and tip-out separately?
  • Does it include wages and the full hours worked?
  • Can you compare jobs, roles or weekdays?
  • Can you correct old entries?
  • Does it explain storage, backup and export behavior?
  • Is the price clear before you build a long history?

When TipHaven may not be the right choice

Do not choose it if you need Android support, employer payroll submission, team scheduling, manager-administered tip pooling or automatic bank connections. TipHaven is intentionally a personal iPhone record and insight tool.

Privacy questions worth asking any app

Tip data can reveal income patterns and workplace details. Read the privacy policy and decide whether you are comfortable with account requirements, cloud storage, analytics and exports.
