TipHaven earnings guide

The best tip tracking app is the one you will still use after a long shift

Look beyond screenshots. Speed, privacy, tip-out handling and useful comparisons determine whether a tracker becomes a real work record.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of TipHaven · Updated November 11, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick answerWhat should a server tip tracker do?

It should make a shift fast to enter, keep gross and net tips distinct, include hours and wages, show true hourly earnings, protect your privacy and let you keep a useful copy of your history.

Six things a server tip tracker should do well

Fast entry

A complete shift should take seconds, not another task after work.

Tip-out

Keep gross tips, tip-out and tips kept separate.

Hourly earnings

Combine wages, net tips and every worked hour.

Useful comparisons

Compare jobs, roles, weekdays and date ranges.

Privacy

Explain accounts, storage, analytics and backups clearly.

Export

Offer a practical way to review or retain the record.

A practical pre-download checklist

  • Can you enter a difficult shift quickly?
  • Does it support the tip-out method your workplace uses?
  • Are opening, closing and side-work hours included?
  • Can you compare several shifts without cherry-picking?
  • Where does the data live?
  • What is the current price and renewal model?

Where TipHaven fits

TipHaven is designed for private iPhone tracking of tips, tip-out, wages, hours, jobs and notes. It calculates net tips, total earnings and true hourly earnings without requiring an account.

Disclosure: Kestora Labs makes TipHaven. This guide states the criteria that favor it and the situations where another product may fit better.

When TipHaven may not be the right choice

Choose another tool if you need Android support, employer payroll integration, multi-user team scheduling, automatic bank import or a cloud account shared across many devices. TipHaven is a personal work record, not payroll software.

Privacy questions worth asking any app

  • Is an account required?
  • Is shift data stored locally or on a server?
  • Is analytics optional?
  • Can the app access location, contacts or financial accounts?
  • How do backup, export and deletion work?
