Tip-out guide

Server tip-out percentage: the base matters as much as the rate.

“We tip out 3%” is incomplete. Three percent of sales and three percent of tips produce very different results.

Updated November 4, 2026 · U.S. federal context with a local-law caution

The essential question3% of what?Ask whether the rate applies to total sales, food sales, alcohol sales, gross tips or another base.

What is a normal server tip-out percentage?

There is no universal percentage.

The workplace, roles receiving the tip-out, calculation base and applicable law all matter.

A lower-looking percentage can cost more when it is applied to a larger base. The only meaningful comparison includes both the percentage and the amount it multiplies.

Two rates that look nothing alike

15% of tips$240 × 15% = $36

The server retains $204 in net tips.

3% of sales$1,800 × 3% = $54

From the same $240 tips, the server retains $186.

Federal and state rules

The U.S. Department of Labor explains that federal tip-pool rules differ depending on whether the employer takes a tip credit. Employers, managers and supervisors generally may not keep employees’ tips, and state law may provide stronger protections.
