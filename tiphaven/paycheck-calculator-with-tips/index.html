What this calculator estimates

wages + overtime wages + tips − tip-out = gross earnings

The optional withholding field applies the percentage you choose to the gross estimate. It is useful for planning, but it is not a tax calculation and does not reproduce a pay stub.

Why tips and your deposited paycheck may look different

Employers report tip income and withhold applicable taxes through payroll, while cash tips may already be in your possession. A pay stub can therefore show taxable tip income without depositing that same tip amount again.

What this estimate does not include

  • State-specific rulesMinimum cash wages and tip-credit rules differ.
  • Exact taxesFiling status, deductions and payroll settings matter.
  • Other deductionsBenefits, meals, uniforms or garnishments are not modeled.

Keep the shift-level record

A payroll estimate is easier to understand when you have your own daily record of tips, tip-out and hours. The IRS says employees generally should keep a daily tip record and report tip income as required.
