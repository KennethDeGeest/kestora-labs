How much do servers make in tips?

There is no honest single tip number. The useful answer combines national wage data with the variables that change from one shift to the next.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of TipHaven · Updated November 11, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick answerHow much do servers make with tips?

BLS reports a $16.93 median hourly wage for waiters and waitresses in the food-services industry in May 2025, including tips. It is a national midpoint—not a promise for a particular server or shift.

Use the national figure as context, not a promise

The BLS industry estimate combines wages and tips across many employers, schedules and regions. Your useful number is what you kept after tip-out plus direct wages, divided by every hour worked.

What changes a server’s tip earnings?

Restaurant

Price point, volume and service style.

Shift

Day, time, section and customer traffic.

Role

Server, bartender and support responsibilities.

Tip-out

The base, rate and eligible recipients.

Location

Local wages, prices and applicable law.

Hours

Side work and waiting time affect the real hourly result.

One shift is not an average

Track several weeks and compare like with like: the same job, role or weekday. Keep unusually large events visible without letting them define the whole pattern.
