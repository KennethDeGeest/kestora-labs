Subscriptions, lifetime purchases and refunds

First try Restore Purchase in TipHaven Settings while signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. Apple manages App Store payments and refund requests. Use Apple’s Report a Problem service for a refund request.

The current TipHaven offer includes a monthly auto-renewable subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings. A valid earlier annual subscription may still be recognized, although it is not offered on the current paywall.
