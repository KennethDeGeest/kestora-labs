Pregnancy scrapbook ideas beyond the usual milestones.

A scrapbook becomes personal when it includes the unremarkable details: the receipt, the playlist, the sentence someone said and the room as it sounded that afternoon.

What can you put in a pregnancy scrapbook?

Mix milestone photos with handwritten notes, appointment cards you are comfortable keeping, name lists, small receipts, fabric, playlists, letters and links to private audio recordings. Avoid including sensitive medical information unless you deliberately want it in the book.

Ten pages that tell more than a timeline

The day before we knewA snapshot of ordinary life just before the story changed.
The first reactionsA line from each person who heard the news.
What was playingSongs, podcasts or stories that filled the background.
Our neighborhoodThe walk, café or view that belonged to this season.
Names we almost choseThe serious, sentimental and wonderfully unlikely options.
Voices waiting for youA QR code or note pointing to a private family recording.

Add sound without turning the book into technology

Keep the physical page simple: title the audio memory, write why it matters and add a private QR code or short retrieval note only if your storage method supports it safely. Test any link periodically; printed links can outlive the service behind them.

A repeatable page recipe

  1. Choose one object or photo.
  2. Add the date or pregnancy stage.
  3. Write two sentences of context.
  4. Add one optional “listen” element.
  5. Leave open space—the page does not need to prove how much happened.
