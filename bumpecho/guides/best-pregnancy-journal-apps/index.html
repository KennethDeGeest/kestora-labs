Our pick for an audio-first pregnancy journal: Bump Echo

Choose Bump Echo if voices, messages and meaningful everyday sounds are the memories you care about. It is deliberately narrower than an all-in-one pregnancy tracker: private local storage, no required account and a one-time lifetime unlock rather than a recurring subscription.

Five questions before you commit

  1. Which memory format matters? Text, photos, audio and timelines serve different purposes.
  2. Can you use it on a tired day? Test the shortest possible entry flow.
  3. Where does personal data live? Read the current privacy policy and account requirements.
  4. What is the payment model? Confirm whether continued use requires a subscription or one-time purchase.
  5. Can you keep or share individual memories? Understand export and backup behavior before building a long history.

When Bump Echo is—and is not—the fit

Bump Echo is the strongest fit when you want to preserve voices without building a public feed or another account. It records ordinary microphone audio, adds titles and notes, and lets you share an individual memory only when you choose.

Choose another tool if your priority is medical information, fetal monitoring, weekly development estimates, symptom tracking, photo-book printing or Android support. Bump Echo is a keepsake, not a health tool.
