A pregnancy journal in the voices that lived it

An audio journal turns tiny recordings into a story you can replay. It works especially well for moments where the sound—not just the fact—is what you want to keep.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of Bump Echo · Updated November 11, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick answerWhat is a pregnancy audio journal?

It is a dated collection of spoken memories and meaningful sounds from pregnancy: reflections, partner messages, family greetings, songs, stories and ordinary moments.

Important: A pregnancy audio journal captures ordinary microphone audio. It does not detect a fetal heartbeat, assess fetal wellbeing or replace any part of prenatal care.

Seven recordings to begin with

  • The story of the day you found out.
  • A short introduction from each parent or caregiver.
  • A grandparent or loved one saying hello.
  • A favorite song sung imperfectly and honestly.
  • The sounds of a room you are preparing.
  • A message from a day that felt difficult or important.
  • A final note during the weeks of waiting.

Name recordings for the memory, not the file

“Voice memo 17” tells you nothing later. Use a human title such as “Dad’s story after dinner,” then add a sentence of context. Dates help build the timeline; titles help you recognize the moment.

Decide who the journal is for

A private journal allows a different kind of honesty from content prepared for social media. Choose whether each recording is for you, your partner, your future child or a wider family circle. Share only the individual moments that belong outside the private collection.
