People before search engines

Each guide must answer a real question, make its assumptions visible and help the reader decide what to do next. Search demand can identify a topic; it does not decide the answer.

Primary sources first

We prioritize public-health bodies, peer-reviewed research, consensus statements and original documentation. Important claims link directly to their sources.

Honest review labels

Content is researched and checked by the Kestora Labs editorial team. We do not describe a guide as medically reviewed unless a qualified medical or dietetic professional has reviewed it.

Clear scope and limitations

The guides are general education for adults, not diagnosis, treatment or an individualized meal prescription. Safety notes identify situations that require professional guidance.

How guides are updated

We review a guide when its sources change, when product behavior changes, when readers identify ambiguity, or when new evidence materially affects the recommendation. The visible update date and structured data are changed only after a meaningful content review.

Corrections and questions

If a claim appears inaccurate, a source no longer supports it, or a safety distinction is unclear, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com. Include the guide URL and the passage in question so it can be checked directly.

BulkBro guides support informed product use and general education. They do not replace advice from a healthcare professional who knows your history.

BulkBro provides general educational planning tools for adults. It is not medical advice, diagnosis or individualized dietetic care.
