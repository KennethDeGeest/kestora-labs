Our approach

StartObserveAdapt

A calculator is the start—not the verdict.

Calorie equations are useful for choosing an initial target. They cannot directly measure your personal maintenance calories, and activity estimates are broad by design. That is why our calculator labels its output as a starting estimate.

Your log creates the useful evidence.

A target only becomes meaningful when you compare it with what you actually ate and how your body weight responded. Consistent food logs and weigh-ins make that comparison easier to interpret.

The plan should be allowed to adapt.

BulkBro is built around a completed seven-day loop: log, weigh in, evaluate, and adjust when the response is different from the intended pace. The guides explain the reasoning; the app helps you repeat it.

These resources provide general educational information for adults and are not medical or dietetic advice. If you have unintentional weight loss, are pregnant, are under 18, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating, speak with a qualified healthcare professional before starting a weight-gain plan.

Turn the estimate into a feedback loop.

BulkBro connects daily calorie logs with your weekly weight response so the target can become more useful over time.
