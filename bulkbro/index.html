03 / The adaptive engine

Your plan should react to you—not the other way around.

Every review connects two signals: what you averaged and how your weight changed. When progress matches the plan, BulkBro holds the target. When it does not, the engine makes a measured adjustment and records why.

Log→Weigh in→Compare→Adapt

06 / Questions

Before you start.

Who is BulkBro for?+

It is designed for people who want to gain weight with a clear calorie, macro and progress-tracking routine—especially hardgainers who are tired of changing plans without enough evidence.

How does food logging work?+

Take or upload a meal photo, describe what you ate out loud, or type it. Review the AI-assisted nutrition estimate and choose whether to add it. Estimates should always be checked before saving.

How does the adaptive engine work?+

BulkBro compares your average calorie intake with your weight response at each review. It keeps a working target steady and makes a measured adjustment when the evidence shows your pace is off track.

Does it replace a dietitian or medical advice?+

No. BulkBro is a planning and tracking tool. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. Speak with a qualified professional when health guidance is needed. Read the full health disclaimer.
