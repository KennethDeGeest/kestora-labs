Support

How can we help?

Help with BulkBro, purchases, feedback and deleting your app data.

Contact support

For questions, bug reports, purchase issues or privacy requests, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, BulkBro version and a short description of what happened.

Email support

App feedback

BulkBro is built around feedback from people trying to gain weight. If something is unclear, broken or missing, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com and tell us what would make the app more useful.

Delete your app data

You can permanently delete your BulkBro data inside the app. Open BulkBro → Settings → Delete data and follow the confirmation steps.

If you no longer have access to the app, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com and we’ll help with your deletion request.
