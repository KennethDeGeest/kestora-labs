BulkBro is listed on the App Store as Bulk Tracker: Gain Weight Plan. Both names refer to the same app.

General Fitness Information

BulkBro is a general fitness and nutrition tracking tool designed to help users pursue intentional weight gain.

BulkBro does not provide medical advice, medical diagnosis, treatment, or professional dietetic care. Nothing in the app should be interpreted as a substitute for advice from a physician, registered dietitian, or other qualified healthcare professional.

Adaptive Calorie Guidance

BulkBro uses information you provide, including your weight, food logs, selected goal, and observed progress, to calculate and adapt calorie targets over time.

These targets are estimates intended for general fitness guidance. Human metabolism varies, and the quality of BulkBro’s guidance depends on the accuracy and consistency of the information you enter.

Weight measurements can fluctuate because of hydration, food intake, glycogen, sodium, measurement timing, and other factors. Calorie tracking can also contain estimation or logging errors. BulkBro cannot guarantee that a calorie target will produce a particular amount or rate of weight gain.

AI Meal Estimates

BulkBro may use artificial intelligence to estimate calories, protein, portions, or other meal information from descriptions you provide.

AI-generated meal estimates can be incomplete or inaccurate. Ingredients, preparation methods, portion sizes, brands, and restaurant recipes can substantially change nutritional values. You are responsible for reviewing meal estimates before relying on them.

Health and Safety

Do not use BulkBro to diagnose or treat being underweight, malnutrition, an eating disorder, a metabolic condition, or any other medical or nutritional condition.

If you are pregnant, under 18, have a medical condition, take medication that may affect weight or metabolism, have a history of disordered eating, or have concerns about whether intentional weight gain is appropriate for you, seek guidance from a qualified healthcare professional before using BulkBro for weight-gain planning.

If you experience concerning symptoms or unexpected changes in your health or weight, stop relying on the app for guidance and contact an appropriate healthcare professional. For an urgent or life-threatening concern, contact local emergency services.

Your Responsibility

You remain responsible for decisions about your diet, calorie intake, exercise, and health. BulkBro provides tools and estimates to support your decision-making; it does not guarantee results.
