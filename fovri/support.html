Support

Help with importing recipes, your cookbook and Fovri Pro.

Updated 4 November 2026

Contact

Fovri is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer (autónomo) based in Spain. For support or privacy requests, contact degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the issue. If needed, send a public source link you are comfortable sharing. Avoid sending private videos, full cookbook backups or unnecessary personal information.

Subscriptions, trial and purchase restoration

Fovri requires an active Fovri Pro subscription after onboarding. The monthly plan is billed immediately. Eligible new subscribers can receive a 14-day free trial on the yearly plan only; Apple determines eligibility and shows the applicable terms before confirmation. Unless cancelled before the trial ends, the yearly subscription renews at the displayed yearly price.

Open Settings → Fovri Pro → View subscription plans. Choose Restore purchases using the Apple Account that bought your subscription. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings. Cancelling or deleting the app does not remove the local cookbook, and deleting the app does not cancel billing. Apple handles refund requests.

An import is incomplete

Some sources do not expose enough recipe information. Check your connection, try importing again, or edit missing ingredients and steps. Extra import help can be enabled or disabled in Settings. AI-inspired recipes should be checked before cooking.

Back up your cookbook

Use Settings to export a cookbook backup before changing devices or removing the app. Keep that file somewhere safe. Restore purchases restores paid access, not your recipes. Your local cookbook cannot be retrieved remotely by support.

Privacy requests

Read the Fovri Privacy Policy or contact the email above to ask about service information we control.
