How to save recipes from Instagram

Saved posts are good for inspiration. An editable cookbook is better for dinner.

Try Fovri on iPhone ↗
INSTAGRAM → DINNERSaved posts are good for inspiration. An editable cookbook is better for dinner.FOVRI KITCHEN GUIDE

QUICK ANSWER

Share or copy the public Instagram Reel or post link into Fovri, review the recipe details gathered from the source, correct anything missing and save it. The original Instagram source remains attached for reference.

In this guide

01 Share the public link02 Turn inspiration into a usable recipe03 What an importer cannot know04 Keep it ready for the right night

01 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

Share the public link

From the public Instagram recipe, use the share menu to send the link to Fovri or copy and paste it into the app. Private, removed or restricted posts may not be available to import.

Only save material you are entitled to access and use, and keep the creator’s source attached.

02 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

Turn inspiration into a usable recipe

  • Give the recipe a specific searchable title
  • Confirm ingredients against the caption and video
  • Write missing method steps in your own words
  • Set a realistic serving count
  • Add your own note after cooking

03 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

What an importer cannot know

A Reel may show a different quantity from its caption, skip preparation steps or assume a familiar technique. No importer can guarantee allergen safety, doneness or accurate nutrition.

Review the original and use authoritative safety guidance whenever temperature or handling matters.

04 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

Keep it ready for the right night

With Fovri, the reviewed recipe joins a private iPhone cookbook rather than another social folder. Add it to Cook next, move missing ingredients to groceries and follow it one step at a time when you cook.
