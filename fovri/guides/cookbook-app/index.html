Why screenshots stop working

Screenshots are quick to capture but difficult to search, scale or turn into a grocery list. Instructions may be split across several images, while the source and serving size disappear.

A cookbook app gives each recipe a stable structure: title, ingredients, method, servings, source and your own edits.

Build around decisions, not categories

  • What can I cook tonight?
  • Which meals use ingredients I already have?
  • What should I add to the grocery list?
  • Which recipes worked well enough to repeat?
  • What needs changing next time?

Keep authorship and food safety visible

A saved recipe remains someone else’s work. Keep the source attached, use imported material personally and follow the creator’s terms.

Automation can misread quantities or steps. Review imports, check allergens and use authoritative food-safety guidance for temperatures and handling.
