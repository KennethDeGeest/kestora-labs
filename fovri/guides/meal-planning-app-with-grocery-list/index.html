Meal planning app with grocery list: one connected flow

Meal planning works better when the plan and list come from the same recipes.

Choose meals you genuinely intend to cook, set the right servings and generate the list from those recipes. Fovri keeps Cook next, serving adjustments and groceries connected so less information is copied by hand.

Why connected planning matters

A calendar of meal names is not enough if the ingredients still have to be copied into a separate list. A connected system knows which recipes are planned and can move their ingredients toward one shopping view.

The result is not perfect automation—it is a better starting point that you can review against the pantry.

A realistic weekly rhythm

  • Choose three or four anchor meals
  • Adjust servings for the people eating
  • Check the pantry before adding ingredients
  • Combine repeated ingredients carefully
  • Leave room for leftovers and changing plans

Review the generated list

Recipe wording is inconsistent: “one onion,” “two small onions” and “200 g onion” may not combine cleanly. Check quantities, remove pantry items and group the list in the way you shop.

Always verify dietary requirements and allergens yourself; a planning app is not a substitute for labels or professional advice.

How Fovri connects the steps

Save a recipe, move it into Cook next, adjust the serving count and add what is missing to groceries. When it is time to cook, the same recipe opens in a focused step view rather than sending you back to the original feed.

From saved idea to dinner
