How to save recipes from TikTok

A bookmark keeps the video. A recipe keeper makes the idea usable when it is time to shop and cook.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of Fovri · Updated November 11, 2026 · 3 min read

Quick answerHow to save recipes from TikTok

Copy or share the public TikTok link to a compatible recipe app such as Fovri. Review the imported title, servings, ingredients and steps, keep the original video attached, then save the corrected recipe to your cookbook.

Save the source first

Open the public TikTok video, use Share and send or copy the link. In Fovri, share the link directly or paste it into the import flow.

The source stays connected so you can return to the creator’s video for visual details that may not appear in the caption.

Review before you save

  • Confirm every ingredient and quantity
  • Add steps that were shown but not written
  • Correct serving size and timing
  • Note substitutions mentioned in the video
  • Check allergens and safe temperatures

Why automatic imports need checking

Short videos often omit quantities, timing or food-safety context. Captions may use shorthand, and on-screen text can be difficult for software to interpret.

Treat an imported recipe as an editable draft, not an authoritative transcription. The cook remains responsible for checking it.

Move beyond the bookmark

Once reviewed in Fovri, the recipe becomes searchable and can move into Cook next, groceries and guided cooking. You keep the original source without needing to scroll through saved videos at dinnertime.
