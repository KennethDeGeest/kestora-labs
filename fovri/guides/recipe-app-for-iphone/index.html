Recipe app for iPhone: what belongs on your home screen

The best iPhone recipe app should feel fast when inspiration appears and quiet when cooking begins.

01 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

Capture should take seconds

On iPhone, the share sheet is the natural bridge between a recipe source and your cookbook. A good app should accept a public link without asking you to retype the title or ingredients.

Fovri accepts shared or pasted public links from supported social platforms and recipe websites, then lets you review the result before saving.

02 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

The interface changes when you cook

  • Large readable text
  • One clear step at a time
  • Relevant ingredients near the method
  • Timers that do not disappear
  • Progress that can be resumed
  • Original source one tap away

03 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

Privacy needs a plain answer

Check the App Store privacy label and the developer’s privacy policy. Find out whether recipes live on your device, in an account or both—and whether analytics or advertising profiles are involved.

Fovri stores the cookbook, plans, groceries, drafts and saved photos locally on the iPhone. Imports require internet access and may use optional cloud assistance.

04 / FOVRI KITCHEN NOTE

A focused iPhone option

Fovri combines share-sheet capture, an editable cookbook, Cook next, groceries, serving adjustments and guided cooking. It is iPhone-first and subscription-based, making it a better fit for frequent users than occasional recipe collectors.
