Questions worth answering first

  • Where do most of your recipes come from?
  • Do you need multiple devices or household sharing?
  • Will you plan meals inside the app?
  • Do you want a guided cooking view?
  • Are you comfortable with a subscription?
  • Can you export or back up your collection?

When an all-in-one app helps

A connected app reduces copying between a social feed, notes, calendar, grocery app and browser. That matters most when you save and cook frequently.

If you only need occasional inspiration, a free recipe website may be enough. Paying for a workflow you will not use does not create value.

Why Fovri is different

Fovri begins where many recipe apps stop: with an idea found on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube or a recipe site. It creates an editable recipe from available information and carries it into a private cookbook, Cook next, groceries and step-by-step cooking.
