Fovri

Privacy Policy

How Fovri handles your cookbook, recipe imports, optional AI assistance, purchases, analytics and support requests.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

Fovri is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer based in Spain. For privacy questions or requests, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Your cookbook

No personal Fovri account is required. Recipes, folders, favourites, meal plans, grocery information, notes and saved photos are stored on your iPhone. Fovri does not operate a cloud cookbook-sync service. Apple device backups may include app data according to your iPhone and iCloud settings.

You control exported cookbook files and any recipes or photos you choose to share.

Recipe imports and optional AI help

When you import a public recipe or cooking-video link, Fovri contacts the source and may process the available page text, captions, images or selected video information needed to create the recipe. Source websites receive ordinary connection information under their own privacy policies.

If optional AI import help is enabled and a normal import needs assistance, the relevant source material is sent securely to service providers that process it for Fovri. Your unrelated cookbook entries, personal notes and photos are not sent for this purpose. Do not import private or sensitive material unless you are comfortable with this processing.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat helps Fovri display offers, confirm subscription access and restore purchases. These services may process purchase, product, entitlement, app and device information. Fovri does not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics and diagnostics

Fovri uses privacy-conscious analytics and crash diagnostics to understand feature use and reliability. This may include app interactions, app version, device and operating-system information, approximate region and crash details. Analytics does not include recipe names, ingredients, grocery text, notes, photos or imported recipe content.

Fovri does not sell personal information, show third-party advertising or use your cookbook to track you across other companies’ apps and websites.

Permissions and notifications

Notifications are optional and may be used for import completion, grocery reminders and cooking timers. Photos or files you select are used only for the action you request. You can manage permissions in Fovri and iPhone Settings.

Retention and deletion

Local cookbook information remains until you delete it or remove the app. Exported files and device backups must be managed where they are stored. Service providers retain limited purchase, analytics, security and diagnostic information according to their settings, contracts and legal obligations. Support correspondence is retained as reasonably needed to answer the request and meet legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability or restriction of personal information under the developer’s control, object to certain processing or withdraw consent. Contact the email above. You may also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency or your local supervisory authority.

Children, security and changes

Fovri is not directed to children under 13. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when Fovri’s features, providers or legal requirements change.
