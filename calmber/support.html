Support

Help with your plan, history, reminders, purchases and privacy.

Updated 11 November 2026

Contact support

For help, feedback, purchase or privacy questions, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com. Include your Calmber version, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Please do not send sensitive health information.

Email support

Your plan and history

Calmber stores your plan, cigarette log and craving history locally on your iPhone. Use the history and editing tools to correct past days or individual entries. Because there is no Calmber account or developer-hosted journal, support cannot remotely recover local history.

If notifications do not appear, check both Calmber’s reminder settings and its notification permission in iOS Settings.

Restore access

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Restoring access does not restore a locally stored quitting journal. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Buying lifetime access does not cancel an existing monthly subscription. Manage or cancel a subscription separately in your Apple Account settings.

Withdrawal, medication and urgent concerns

Calmber provides behavioral support, not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It does not replace a doctor, pharmacist, cessation specialist or emergency service.

Contact a qualified healthcare professional for withdrawal concerns, cessation medication, pregnancy or breastfeeding questions, mental-health concerns, or a quit plan suited to your conditions and medicines. If you believe you may be experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency service.

Privacy questions

Your detailed quitting journal stays in Calmber’s local app storage. Read the Calmber Privacy Policy for details about local information, analytics and purchase services.
