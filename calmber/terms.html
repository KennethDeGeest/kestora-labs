What Calmber does

Calmber is a personal behavioral-support app for adults who want to reduce or stop smoking. It can create a gradual taper plan, record cigarettes and cravings, show estimates and patterns, guide short coping exercises, schedule optional reminders and represent progress through a lung-buddy character.
