Calmber

Privacy Policy

How Calmber handles your plan, cigarette and craving history, purchases and limited technical information.

Effective 11 November 2026

Who provides Calmber

Calmber is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer based in Spain. Privacy and support questions may be sent to degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Calmber stores the information you enter and the history needed to operate your quit plan. This can include your smoking baseline, taper pace, wake and bedtime, day boundary, pack price, pack size and currency; cigarette timestamps and optional triggers; craving intensity, triggers, selected exercises and outcomes; daily limits, plan changes, recovery progress, notification preferences and other app settings.

This detailed quitting journal is stored in the app’s local storage on your iPhone. Calmber does not require an account and does not upload that journal to a Kestora Labs-operated server.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Calmber uses Apple’s local notification system for reminders and milestones you enable. You can change permission in iOS Settings and reminder choices inside Calmber.

Analytics and purchases

Calmber uses Firebase Analytics to understand broad app activity such as onboarding completion, paywall views, purchase and restore outcomes, cigarette-log actions, craving sessions, plan adjustments and review prompts. Calmber is configured without advertising-ID support. Analytics events do not include your cigarette count, craving intensity, selected trigger, pack price, currency, notification times or free-form content.

Calmber uses RevenueCat to retrieve product offerings and verify purchase and entitlement status, and Apple to process StoreKit transactions, billing, refunds and purchase restoration. These providers may process pseudonymous identifiers, app and device information, product identifiers, purchase events and access status under their own policies.

Calmber does not receive full payment-card details, sell personal information, or use your app activity to track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

How information is used

Information is processed to create and adapt your plan, show progress and estimates, provide craving exercises, schedule reminders you request, verify paid access, restore purchases, understand broad feature use, maintain reliability, provide support and improve Calmber.

Retention and deletion

Your local quitting journal remains on your device until you edit or delete entries, reset relevant information, or remove the app. Removing the app normally removes its local container, although Apple device or iCloud backups may retain app data depending on your settings.

Firebase, RevenueCat and Apple retain limited service information according to their own policies and legal obligations. Because Calmber has no user account or developer-hosted quitting journal, the developer cannot remotely access or delete information that exists only on your device.

Security, international processing and age

Calmber relies on iOS storage and platform security features intended to protect local information. No storage or transmission method is guaranteed completely secure. Service providers may process limited technical or purchase information outside your country under their safeguards and applicable law.

Calmber is intended for adults. It is not directed to children under 13. If local law requires a higher minimum age to use a smoking-cessation service independently, that higher age applies.

Your choices and rights

You can edit past days and individual entries, change your plan and notification preferences, deny notifications, remove the app, restore purchases and manage a monthly subscription through your Apple Account settings.

For a privacy request or question, contact degeestkenneth@gmail.com. Requests involving Apple, Firebase or RevenueCat information may also need to be directed to that provider.

Changes

This policy may be updated when Calmber’s features, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
