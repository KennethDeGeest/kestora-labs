Capture a small fact in the moment, then return later for details before, during and after the headache phase.

Quick answerHow is this different from a monthly diary?

A monthly diary shows frequency at a glance. An episode worksheet gives one event room for timing, symptoms, medication, impact and notes across phases.

A before, during and after episode record

Before

Date, context and any symptoms noticed before head pain.

During

Start time, severity, symptoms, medication and immediate impact.

After

Approximate end, lingering symptoms, recovery and anything to discuss.

Separate observation from recollection

Migraine experiences can change across an episode. Record what you know at the time, then label details added later as recollection. The phases organize memory; they do not determine a diagnosis.

Use the template without demanding a perfect entry

  • Capture only date, time and severity when symptoms are active.
  • Return later for optional detail.
  • Leave unknown information blank.
  • Use neutral descriptions rather than assigning a cause.
  • Bring the record to a qualified professional when useful.

Editorial note: This guide is general information, not medical advice. It was reviewed against the sources below. A diary can support a healthcare conversation; it cannot determine a diagnosis or treatment.
