Why the template separates phases

Migraine can involve more than headache pain. The American Migraine Foundation describes prodrome, aura, headache and postdrome phases, while noting that not everyone experiences every phase. The NHS also lists early symptoms and aura among possible parts of an attack.

The headings above are prompts, not labels you must apply. Record what you experienced in your own words; leave clinical interpretation to a qualified professional.

A low-burden way to use it

  1. Write the date and approximate start when you can.
  2. During active symptoms, record only the smallest useful fact.
  3. Return later for duration, impact and relief notes.
  4. Distinguish what you recorded at the time from what you remember later.
  5. Bring the sheet to a healthcare conversation if it helps you explain your history.
