How to make the calendar useful

  1. Define what your severity codes mean to you.
  2. Mark entries consistently rather than reconstructing a month from memory.
  3. Keep a separate episode sheet when one day needs more context.
  4. Review totals and questions before an appointment; do not treat a visual pattern as proof of cause.

What a compact tracker leaves out

A calendar is good at frequency and rough severity. It is poor at showing duration, phases, symptoms, treatment timing and functional impact. If those details matter, pair it with an episode log or an app. A blank day must also remain distinct from a confirmed symptom-free day.
