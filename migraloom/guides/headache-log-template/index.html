Event log or daily diary?

An event log records only headache episodes. A daily diary can distinguish recorded symptom-free days from days with no entry. Choose the level you can maintain and explain what blanks mean.

Why these five fields?

DatePlaces the episode in the wider history.
Start and durationSeparates when it began from how long it lasted.
SeverityUses one consistent personal scale across entries.
Medication and timeRecords what you took; it does not advise what to take.
Symptoms or impactCaptures the detail most useful to remember later.
Blank meaningPrevents missing records from being treated as symptom-free days.

When a simple log works best

Use an event log when your priority is remembering episodes rather than completing a daily check-in. Choose a monthly diary when frequency and confirmed symptom-free days matter. Choose the episode worksheet when one event needs more context.
