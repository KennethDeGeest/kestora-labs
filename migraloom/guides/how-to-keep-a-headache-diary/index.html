A useful headache diary does not need to become a second job

Start with the question you want the record to answer, then track the smallest set of details you can maintain honestly.

Quick answerWhat is the smallest useful headache diary?

A date, approximate timing, severity and one short note can preserve useful context. Add fields only when they serve a clear purpose.

What a headache diary is for

A diary can preserve frequency, duration, severity, symptoms, medication use and functional impact for a healthcare conversation. It does not prove causation or diagnose a headache type.

How to keep a headache diary in five steps

  • Choose one purpose, such as remembering timing or preparing for an appointment.
  • Pick the smallest useful fields.
  • Define what blanks mean.
  • Record near the event when possible.
  • Review with questions, not conclusions.

A minimum entry for a difficult day

Use: date · approximate time · severity · one note. For example: “Nov 5 · began around 14:00 · 7/10 · light sensitivity; medication recorded at 14:20.”

Review before an appointment

  • Choose the relevant date range.
  • Separate recorded headache days from unrecorded days.
  • Note changes you want to discuss.
  • Bring a current medication list and questions.
  • Keep the raw record available.

Editorial note: This guide is general information, not medical advice. It was reviewed against the sources below. A diary can support a healthcare conversation; it cannot determine a diagnosis or treatment.
