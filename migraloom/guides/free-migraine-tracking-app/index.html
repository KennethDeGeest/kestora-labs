“Free download” is not one business model

It can mean unlimited basic logging, a limited number of entries, a timed trial, a recurring subscription for advanced reports, or a combination. The useful question is not only “Can I download it for free?” but “What remains usable after the free period?”

Four common free-app models

Free core

Basic logging stays free while advanced insights or reports require payment.

Entry allowance

You can test the full flow for a fixed number of episodes before continued logging is paid.

Timed trial

Premium access is available for a short period and may convert into a subscription.

Freemium subscription

Some functions remain free; a monthly or annual plan unlocks the rest.

Lifetime option

A one-time purchase may exist alongside, or instead of, subscriptions.

Completely free

Check how the app is funded and review its current privacy information.

Five questions to answer first

  • Can I keep logging?
    Find the exact free entry or time limit.
  • Can I still read my history?
    A paywall for new entries is different from losing access to existing records.
  • Are reports and export included?
    These often sit in a paid tier.
  • Does a trial renew automatically?
    Review the App Store confirmation screen and subscription terms.
  • Is there a one-time option?
    Compare the lifetime price with the period you expect to use the tracker.

How MigraLoom’s free allowance works

MigraLoom currently lets you record five complete episodes. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks continued episode recording. Existing history, Patterns, Reports and basic export remain available without that purchase. It does not use an auto-renewing subscription.

This model is a good fit if you want to test the actual episode flow and prefer a one-time unlock. It is not the right fit if you need unlimited free logging.
