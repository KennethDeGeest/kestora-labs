“Free” matters most after you have built a history

Before choosing a tracker, find out which actions stay free, what happens to existing records and whether export remains available.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of MigraLoom · Updated November 11, 2026 · 1 minute read

Quick answerWhat does “free migraine tracker” actually mean?

It may mean unlimited basic logging, a limited number of entries, a timed trial or free logging with paid reports. Check what remains usable after the free period.

Four common free-app models

Unlimited basic

Core logging stays free while advanced tools are paid.

Entry allowance

A fixed number of complete entries is free.

Timed trial

Paid features are available for a limited period.

Freemium reports

Logging is free, while summaries or exports require payment.

Five questions before you build a history

  • How many entries can I create for free?
  • Can I still read old entries after a limit is reached?
  • Is export included or locked behind payment?
  • Does the app require an account or cloud storage?
  • Is the paid path recurring or one-time?

How MigraLoom’s free allowance works

MigraLoom lets you complete five episodes before continued logging requires a one-time unlock. Existing entries remain part of your local history. There is no required account or recurring subscription.

Pricing and included features can change. Confirm the current App Store purchase screen before choosing.
