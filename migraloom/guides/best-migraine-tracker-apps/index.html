Quick answerWhich migraine tracker is the best fit?

Choose MigraLoom for focused local iPhone tracking with no required account or recurring subscription. Choose a broader alternative when integrations or multi-condition tools matter more.

Five criteria that matter after the first week

Logging burden

Can you record the minimum on a difficult day?

Privacy

Is an account required, and where is the history stored?

Review

Can you revisit frequency, severity and duration?

Export

Can you bring a useful copy to an appointment?

Payment

Is continued use free, subscription-based or a one-time purchase?

Choose around your real purpose

  • Test the shortest logging flow on a difficult day.
  • Read the current privacy information and account requirements.
  • Check whether exports remain available if you stop paying.
  • Confirm the exact current pricing in your storefront.
  • Choose the record you can maintain—not the longest feature list.

What this comparison cannot tell you

This is not a clinical evaluation or security audit. App Store descriptions and privacy labels are developer-provided, availability differs by country and features change.

Editorial note: This guide is general information, not medical advice. It was reviewed against the sources below. A diary can support a healthcare conversation; it cannot determine a diagnosis or treatment.
