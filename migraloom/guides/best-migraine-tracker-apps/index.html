How to choose without overthinking it

  1. Decide whether you want another subscription. If the answer is no, MigraLoom is the clearest fit in this comparison.
  2. Start with your real purpose. Are you recording attacks, tracking several conditions, exploring context or preparing for appointments?
  3. Test the worst-day flow. If logging feels demanding when symptoms are active, the rest of the feature list may not matter.
  4. Read the privacy information. Decide whether you are comfortable with accounts, syncing, integrations and how exports work.
  5. Check the exit path. Look for PDF, CSV or another useful way to keep a copy of your history.
  6. Verify current pricing. App Store offers change. Review the exact purchase screen before building a long record.

What this comparison cannot tell you

We did not clinically evaluate these apps, audit their source code, test every paid feature or rank medical effectiveness. App Store descriptions are developer-provided. Privacy labels and listing claims are useful starting points, not independent security audits. Availability and features can also differ by country.

The best practical test is to try the logging flow, read the current privacy policy and decide whether the app’s record would be useful in the conversations you actually have.
