The best migraine diary is the one that survives your real routine

Paper is immediate and visible. Apps make history easier to search and summarize. A hybrid works only when it avoids double entry.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of MigraLoom · Updated November 11, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick answerIs paper or an app medically better?

Neither format is inherently superior. Consistency, clarity and usefulness to you and your healthcare conversations matter more than the medium.

Paper, app or hybrid?

Printable diary

Best for handwriting, a visible sheet and flexible notes.

Tracker app

Best for searchable history, summaries, reminders and export.

Hybrid

Best when paper is easiest during an episode but digital review is useful later.

Five questions to choose a format

  • What will be within reach during symptoms?
  • Do you need confirmed symptom-free days?
  • Do you want manual review or date-range summaries?
  • How will you bring the record to an appointment?
  • Which privacy and backup tradeoffs are acceptable?

Use a hybrid without double entry

Use paper for a minimal timestamp or bedside note, then transfer only essential detail to the primary digital record. Mark it as later transcription and avoid maintaining two complete histories that can silently disagree.

Editorial note: This guide is general information, not medical advice. It was reviewed against the sources below. A diary can support a healthcare conversation; it cannot determine a diagnosis or treatment.
