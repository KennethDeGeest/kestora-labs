Headache Tracker: MigraLoom

Privacy Policy

How MigraLoom handles your local headache journal, exports, purchases, optional diagnostics and support requests.

Updated 14 November 2026

Who is responsible

MigraLoom is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer based in Spain. For privacy questions or rights requests, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com. This policy describes MigraLoom 1.2 and later; earlier versions may have different purchase screens.

Your journal and backups

No MigraLoom account is required. Episodes, symptoms, medicines and doses, notes, daily check-ins, profile answers, treatment changes, appointment questions and unfinished drafts are stored on your iPhone. MigraLoom does not operate cloud journal sync. Journal contents are not sent to Firebase or RevenueCat. The journal uses iOS file protection and may be included in Apple device backups according to your settings. Apple controls its backup service and retention.

Purchases, paywalls and RevenueCat

Paid access is required after onboarding: an active monthly subscription or a valid lifetime purchase. Existing valid entitlements remain recognized. Apple processes payments; we do not receive payment-card details. RevenueCat supplies the paywall and verifies, synchronizes and restores purchase access. It may process an app-user identifier, product and transaction identifiers, receipts, purchase history, entitlement and renewal status, app/device information, connection information and country inferred from IP, and paywall impressions and purchase interactions. These purchase services operate separately from optional Firebase diagnostics. Journal contents are not included.

Optional Firebase diagnostics

Share optional product diagnostics is off by default and is not required to buy or use paid features. Change it in Privacy & data on the paywall or Settings → Privacy in the app. With consent, Google Firebase Analytics receives limited product events: paywall/sample views, plan loading, the first saved record, Patterns opens, purchase and restore outcomes, and optional fixed-choice feedback after abandoning a purchase. No free-text feedback or journal details are attached. The sample onboarding interaction is illustrative and does not save an episode.

Firebase may also receive automatic app/session and App Store transaction signals, product and price/currency information, an installation or app-instance identifier, app/device and operating-system details, timestamps and technical crash traces through Crashlytics. Automatic screen-view reporting is disabled. Event timing and app use can still reveal use of a migraine app. We do not request precise location for diagnostics or attach symptoms, medication names or free-form health notes to events.

While diagnostics are enabled, the Firebase app-instance identifier is shared with RevenueCat to associate diagnostics with purchase activity. Turning diagnostics off stops future Analytics collection and Crashlytics uploads and marks that identifier for removal from RevenueCat attributes. It does not recall information already received. Crash reports retained on the phone while collection is off may be sent if you enable diagnostics later.

Exports, deletion and access

You choose where to share visit-summary PDFs or complete-journal JSON exports; these may contain sensitive health information and unfinished drafts. Copies and Apple backups must be managed at their destination. Delete local journal in Settings removes local journal and app-managed recovery/export/report copies. Deleting the app removes app-container data, not copies already shared or Apple backups. We cannot remotely read or recover your local journal. Subscription expiry, refund or revocation may block journal features without deleting records. Export records you need while you have access; contact support for access or data-request difficulties. Deleting records or the app does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Purposes, legal bases and retention

Optional diagnostics rely on consent to understand product use and troubleshoot faults. Purchase processing supports the agreement to provide, verify and restore paid access. Necessary security and service-reliability processing and support may rely on legitimate interests; legally required records are kept to meet legal obligations. We do not sell journal data or use it for advertising. Local records remain until you delete them. Diagnostic data follows the configured Firebase product retention and deletion processes. Purchase records may be retained to restore entitlements, resolve disputes and meet legal obligations. Support correspondence is kept as needed for the request and applicable obligations. Contact us about provider-held data we control and any required retention.

Providers and international processing

Providers include Apple for App Store purchases and device backups, RevenueCat for paywalls and purchase access, Google Firebase for optional diagnostics, Gmail for support and GitHub Pages for this website. They may process data outside Spain and the EEA under applicable transfer safeguards, including adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where required. Website analytics consent is separate from the app’s diagnostics choice; see Website privacy below. Provider information is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy, revenuecat.com/privacy and apple.com/legal/privacy. Contact us for information about relevant safeguards.

Your rights and choices

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Withdraw diagnostics consent in the app without affecting your purchase or earlier lawful processing. Email degeestkenneth@gmail.com with only the information necessary to identify your request; do not send health records unnecessarily. We may request proportionate verification and normally respond within one month, explaining any lawful extension. You may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) or your local supervisory authority. Local-only data is controlled through your device.

Children, health and policy changes

MigraLoom is not directed to children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child supplied personal information. MigraLoom is a journal, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or an emergency service. We use reasonable safeguards but cannot guarantee absolute security. Policy changes appear here with an updated date; material changes and any new consent will be communicated where required.
