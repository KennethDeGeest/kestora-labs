Headache Tracker: MigraLoom

Privacy Policy

How MigraLoom handles your local headache journal, exports, purchases, optional diagnostics and support requests.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

MigraLoom is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer based in Spain. For privacy questions or requests, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com.

Your headache journal

No personal account is required. Episodes, symptoms, triggers, medication notes and related journal information are stored on your iPhone. MigraLoom does not operate a cloud journal. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings.

Exports and sharing

You choose whether to create or share an export. Exported files can contain sensitive health information. Store and share them carefully. Copies already sent to another person or service are governed by that destination.

Purchases and optional diagnostics

Apple processes App Store payments and purchase services help verify or restore access. If you enable optional diagnostics, limited app, device, performance and crash information may be processed to improve reliability. Journal entries, symptom notes and medication details are not intentionally included in diagnostics.

Retention and deletion

Journal information remains on your device until you delete it or remove the app. Device backups and exported files must be managed separately. Providers retain limited purchase and diagnostic information under their own retention rules and legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You can keep diagnostics disabled, edit or delete journal entries, remove the app and control exports. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection for personal information under the developer’s control. Contact the email above.

Health notice, security and changes

MigraLoom is a personal journal, not a medical device, diagnosis or substitute for professional care. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no system can guarantee absolute security. This policy may be updated when features, providers or legal requirements change.
