06 / QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Is MigraLoom a medical device or diagnostic tool?+

No. MigraLoom is a personal journal and appointment-preparation tool. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, treatment or emergency care.

Can I use the app without creating an account?+

Yes. No MigraLoom account is required to use the journal.

What happens after five episodes?+

You can unlock continued episode recording with a one-time in-app purchase. Existing history and the basic review tools remain available.

Does MigraLoom determine my triggers?+

No. It helps you review recurring details in what you recorded, but it does not infer causes or make medical conclusions.

Can I share my history with a healthcare professional?+

You can prepare a structured visit summary and export a PDF when you decide to share it.
