Headache Tracker: MigraLoom

Migraine Diary & Symptom Log

A calm, private migraine journal for capturing episodes, understanding recorded patterns, and preparing clearer conversations with healthcare professionals.

View on the App Store ↗Browse guides →

The idea

Remember less. Bring a clearer record.

Migraine days make detailed note-taking difficult, and important context is easy to forget later. MigraLoom makes the first entry fast, then lets users add detail when they are ready and turn the record into something useful.

What it does

  • Record an episode quicklyStart with timing and intensity, then add symptoms, pain location, medication, and notes when it feels manageable.
  • See only recorded patternsSummarize frequency, duration, intensity, and recurring context without presenting guesses as medical conclusions.
  • Prepare for appointmentsCreate a structured visit summary and export a PDF only when the user chooses to share it.
