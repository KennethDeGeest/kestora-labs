Support

Help with MigraLoom, purchases and privacy.

Updated 5 November 2026

Contact support

For help, feedback, purchase or privacy questions, email degeestkenneth@gmail.com. Include your app version, iOS version and a short description of the problem. Please do not send journal exports, medical records or sensitive health information unless necessary and specifically discussed.

Email support

Restore a purchase

Use Restore purchases in MigraLoom with the Apple Account used to buy the unlock. Apple handles billing and refund requests.

Your journal

Use the app’s export controls to keep an independent copy. Delete local journal removes app-managed local records and recovery/export copies, but cannot remove backups or copies shared elsewhere. We cannot remotely retrieve a journal from your phone.

Medical questions

MigraLoom support cannot provide medical advice or emergency assistance. Contact a qualified health professional for care. Contact local emergency services in an emergency.
