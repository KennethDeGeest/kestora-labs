Better identifications

Photograph the subject in natural light and keep it sharp and centered. Start with the whole tree or plant, then add a clear close-up of a leaf, flower, bark, grain or cut surface from the same discovery.

Never rely on an app result for edibility, toxicity, medical, fire or structural-safety decisions.

Purchases & subscriptions

Use Restore Purchases if an active subscription is not recognized. Apple handles payment, cancellation and refund decisions. You can manage a subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Privacy & deletion

Discoveries, photos, notes and precise pins are stored locally. Use Settings to delete all app data, or remove individual discoveries from your collection. See the Privacy Policy for online processing details.
