What Oqvi does

Oqvi uses submitted photos to suggest trees, plants, leaves, bark, flowers and wood; provides educational context and AI-assisted answers; and lets you save discoveries, photos, notes, favorites, exports and private map locations.

Identification and safety limitations

Identification and AI results are automated suggestions and may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Similar species, image quality, regional variation, processed timber, seasonal changes and incomplete context can reduce accuracy.

Do not rely on Oqvi to decide whether something is edible, poisonous, allergenic, invasive, protected, suitable for medicine, safe to burn, structurally sound or appropriate for construction or land management. Confirm important decisions with a qualified local expert and authoritative sources. If exposure or poisoning may have occurred, contact an appropriate medical professional, poison service or emergency service immediately.
