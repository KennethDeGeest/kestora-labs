Privacy Policy

How Oqvi handles photos used for identification, your private collection, purchases, diagnostics and support requests.

Last updated 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

Oqvi is provided by Kenneth Marcel S De Geest, a self-employed developer based in Spain. Contact degeestkenneth@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.

Your private collection

Saved discoveries, notes, favourites and precise map pins are stored on your iPhone. Oqvi does not operate an account-based cloud collection. Apple device backups may include app data according to your settings.

Plant-identification requests

When you request an identification, the selected photo and limited context needed for the request are sent securely to service providers that process the identification for Oqvi. Results are suggestions and may be inaccurate. Do not submit private or sensitive images.

Location, photos and permissions

Camera, photo-library and location permissions are requested only for features you choose. Precise saved map pins remain in your local collection. You can review or revoke permissions in iPhone Settings.

Purchases, analytics and diagnostics

Apple processes App Store payments, and purchase services help verify access and restore purchases. Oqvi may use limited analytics and crash diagnostics to understand reliability and general feature use. This information does not include your private collection, notes or precise saved locations.

Retention and deletion

Local collection data remains until you delete it or remove the app. Device backups and exported copies must be managed separately. Providers retain limited purchase, security, analytics and diagnostic information under their own retention rules and legal obligations.

Your rights, security and changes

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing of personal information under the developer’s control. Contact the email above. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no system is completely secure. This policy may change when Oqvi’s features, providers or legal requirements change.
