Free should describe the whole experience

A free download can mean unlimited identification, one free result, a trial or a built-in phone feature. Here is how to tell the difference.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of Oqvi · Updated November 11, 2026 · 2 minute read

Quick answerIs Oqvi a free plant identifier?

Oqvi is free to download and includes one free identification. Additional photo identifications and AI questions use a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription.

Four meanings of “free”

Unlimited free

Identification remains available without payment.

Free allowance

A small number of results is included before payment.

Timed trial

Paid access starts after a trial unless cancelled.

Built-in feature

The phone supplies occasional supported lookups without another app.

Check these details before tapping subscribe

  • How many identifications remain free?
  • Does the trial renew automatically?
  • Can you still view saved discoveries without paying?
  • Is an account required?
  • What data is uploaded for identification?
  • Can you export or delete your collection?

Oqvi’s model, plainly

Oqvi includes one free identification. Continued photo identification and AI follow-up questions require a monthly or yearly subscription. Your storefront shows the current price and renewal terms before purchase.

Choose around your real use

For occasional supported photos, start with Apple Visual Look Up. For unlimited community identification, consider Pl@ntNet or Seek. Choose Oqvi when a private, organized field collection and botanical follow-up questions justify the paid path.
