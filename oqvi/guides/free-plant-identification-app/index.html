Pricing guide

Free should describe the whole experience.

A free download can mean unlimited identification, one free result, a trial, or a built-in phone feature. Here is how to tell the difference.

Published by Kestora Labs, the maker of Oqvi · Updated November 5, 2026

FIELD NOTE / 02Free is not one modelRead the purchase screen

Field index

Quick answerOqvi in the fieldSourcesRelated guides
Oqvi is free to download and includes one free identification.

Additional photo identifications and AI questions use a monthly or yearly auto-renewable subscription. If you need unlimited no-cost identification, Pl@ntNet, Seek or a supported iPhone Visual Look Up result may suit you better.

Four meanings of “free”

01

Free service

The core identification feature is available without a recurring payment, although accounts or community sharing may still be involved.

02

Free allowance

You receive a fixed number of results before deciding whether the paid version is useful.

03

Free trial

Paid access starts after an eligible trial unless cancelled under the terms shown before purchase.

04

Built in

Your phone may recognize supported subjects from photos without installing a dedicated plant app.

Check these details before tapping subscribe

  • Whether the trial converts automatically and when it renews
  • Whether the advertised feature works without creating an account
  • Which identification types are included
  • Whether saved photos, notes or locations are uploaded
  • How to cancel and restore a purchase through your Apple Account

Oqvi’s model, plainly

Oqvi lets you try the core identification flow once without paying. Monthly and yearly subscriptions unlock additional photo identifications and follow-up AI questions. Current localized prices, renewal terms and any trial eligibility appear in Oqvi and on Apple’s confirmation sheet before you buy.

Your saved collection does not require a Kestora Labs account. Discoveries, notes and precise map locations are stored locally on your iPhone, while submitted identification photos and questions require online processing.

Choose around your real use

If you identify something once every few months, Apple Visual Look Up may be enough when it recognizes the photo. For free plant-focused identification with ranked possibilities, consider Pl@ntNet. For broad nature exploration without an account, consider Seek. Choose Oqvi when a private, searchable record of plants, bark and wood is worth keeping after the result.

FIELD SAFETY

Identification results can be incomplete or wrong. Never rely on a photo or AI answer for edibility, toxicity, medical care, fire safety, structural use or another consequential decision. Confirm with a qualified expert.
