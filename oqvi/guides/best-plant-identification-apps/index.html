When Oqvi is—and is not—the fit

Oqvi fits repeated private discoveries on iPhone: identify a candidate, ask a follow-up question and keep photos, notes, favorites and optional places together. Choose another option when you need unlimited no-cost identification, a public community, Android support or wildlife coverage beyond botanical materials.

Disclosure: Kestora Labs makes Oqvi. App listings and offers change; verify current features, pricing and privacy information before choosing.

Treat every result as a suggestion

Photo identification can narrow possibilities but cannot certify identity, safety or suitability. Compare several independent features with a regional key or qualified expert when the answer matters.
